Meditate At Ease

Meditation Mobile-Application

UX CASE STUDY

Project: Designed a meditation mobile-application that offers variety of meditation sessions guiding users on a journey towards calmness and tranquility.

Project Type: Individual

Duration: 4 months

Tools used: Figma, Photoshop

Objective

The objective of designing the mobile-application Meditate At Ease is to provide a simple and intuitive platform that enables users to meditate anytime anywhere easily. The application provides a diverse range of meditation techniques with varying experience levels - beginner, intermediate and advanced. Users can set reminders as well to maintain consistency in their mindfulness journey. Meditate At Ease aims to ensure an accessible and seamless user-experience is provided, promoting a peaceful and balanced state of mind for all.

Problem Statement & Project Goals

How might we elevate the Rock Paper Scissors website to enable effortless navigation while delivering a more engaging and cohesive brand experience?

Enhance Brand Identity

Use playful boutique colors, maintain consistent typography and visuals, and showcase unique products to create a strong and memorable brand presence.

Increase Engagement

Update banners seasonally, showcase testimonials and favorite and introduce playful interactive features to keep users engaged.

Improve Experience

Create a friendly and interactive experience by simplifying navigation, streamlining checkout and adding a clear progress indicator (Shipping → Payment → Confirmation).

Align Architecture

Restructure navigation with clear hierarchy, group related content for easy discovery and maintain consistent layouts across all pages.

Research

To understand current market trends and user expectations, we conducted research on similar local businesses and competitors, uncovering patterns in navigation, engagement, brand presentation, and site architecture

Competitor Analysis

To understand current market trends and user expectations, we analyzed competitors like Catching Fireflies and Found Gallery. Our research uncovered patterns in navigation, user engagement, brand presentation, and site architecture. These insights directly informed the goals for the Rock Paper Scissors redesign, helping us prioritize intuitive user flows, playful interactions, and a cohesive brand experience.

Design Iterations

Improvements made after testing the low-fidelity prototype of Meditate At Ease.

01
Text-to-Speech Option

Users wanted hands-free meditation without constantly checking the screen.

Added text-to-speech for all meditation techniques to assist visually impaired users and enhance convenience.

02
Reminder Confirmation

Users experienced issues transitioning to the reminder confirmation page.

Fixed navigation so users receive a clear confirmation after setting reminders.

Transformative Meditation with AR/VR

An idea for incorporating AR/VR into Meditate At Ease - Meditation app

A visualize button for each meditation technique/session can be provided. By clicking this visualize button and using a VR headset, users can be transported into relaxing and pleasant environments which will allow users to disconnect from the surroundings and engage more deeply with the meditation techniques given. This immersive meditation practice will enable users to connect with their inner selves and be in a calm state of mind.
